LLM Evals FAQ · Husain & Shankar, 2026
🔍 Error analysis first, infra second. Review 20–50 real outputs manually before building any automated eval.
👑 Benevolent indicator. One subject matter expert sets the quality bar — nurses and MDs, not committee vote.
✅ Binary pass/fail beats Likert. 1–5 ratings are subjective. Pass/fail forces clarity.
🏥 Clinical = false negatives cost more. Missing something is worse than over-flagging.
The Three Gulfs
1 Comprehension — understand your data before writing a prompt
2 Specification — observations into a clear, testable rubric
3 Generalization — what works on 10 can fail on 10,000

Lessons Learned · Personal + Work

What the journey
actually taught me.

1
Start with the problem. Always.
Every tool I regret was adopted because it was impressive, not because it solved something real.
2
Evaluation is non-negotiable.
Golden dataset at work. "Does this save time?" personally. Without it you're flying blind.
3
Things move fast. That's the point.
The tools change; the mindset doesn't. OpenClaw → Hermes in months.
